About Social Circles

Say yes
to something new

Jersey is full of good things happening, but the smaller events often slip past unheard. Social Circles brings them into one place, so more people turn up and more business reaches the island's independent organisers, venues and makers.

That's where the name comes from. Every corner of Jersey has its own circle: the makers, the growers, the venues, the brands, the people who love trying new things. We're the layer that brings those circles together, so together we can create something new, and even better, than any of us would alone. If you're curious, up for something new and want to see more happen here, come and join us.

Our visionDifferent circles, brought together. Unique partnerships and experiences that back the island's independents and give curious people something worth showing up for.
  • 1DiscoverThe smaller events you'd usually only hear about afterwards.
  • 2SupportMore people through the doors of local businesses.
  • 3GatherOur own series with local partners: on the land, on the move, close to home, or making something together.
